# P28 — Cross-Semester Transfer Validation, Prior Calibration & Longitudinal Learning Profile

## Objective

P27 lets archived evidence guide what to verify first in a new semester. P28 tests whether that guidance was actually useful once fresh evidence exists.

The operating principle is:

> historical priors may influence diagnostic attention; only current-semester evidence may establish current mastery.

P28 therefore adds a derived, read-only analytical layer. It does not create another mastery store, does not schedule reviews, and does not mutate course readiness.

## Evidence contract

Each historical prior is evaluated against fresh evidence from the target course.

Historical signal comes only from the immutable P27 prior snapshot:

- latest official outcome;
- latest official score;
- latest archived readiness index / band;
- unresolved archived reconciliation findings.

Current signal comes from:

- a completed P12 baseline, when one exists;
- independent attempts completed during the first 21 days of the target semester.

Guided attempts never count as early transfer evidence.

The early-attempt signal requires at least four independent attempts before it is considered usable.

## Signal model

Both historical and current evidence are reduced to a bounded course-level signal:

- positive;
- mixed;
- negative;
- unknown.

A prior is then classified as:

- confirmed — historical and current directional signals agree;
- partial — one side is mixed;
- contradicted — historical and current directional signals disagree;
- insufficient_evidence — one side is still unknown.

The model deliberately does not claim causality, pass probability, or grade prediction.

## Prior calibration

P28 aggregates transfer outcomes by P27 relation type:

- direct_retake;
- prerequisite;
- related.

Reliability is calculated only from usable transitions. Insufficient cases are reported but excluded from the denominator.

Confirmed transitions receive full credit, partial transitions half credit, and contradicted transitions no credit.

This creates an empirical answer to a narrow question:

> when this type of prior has been attached before, how often did its directional signal remain useful after fresh evidence arrived?

No reliability score can override current mastery.

## Longitudinal learning profile

Durable course-level patterns are derived only from direct-retake transitions that preserve stable course identity.

Possible patterns are:

- insufficient_evidence;
- emerging;
- durable_strength;
- recurring_weakness;
- context_sensitive.

At least two usable cross-semester transitions are required before StudyOS labels a strength, weakness, or context-sensitive pattern as durable.

Prerequisite and related priors contribute to relation calibration but never create a same-course longitudinal trait.

## Progress integration

The Progress screen now surfaces:

- active historical-prior count;
- usable transfer validations;
- confirmed / contradicted / insufficient counts;
- relation-level reliability;
- per-course source → current signal;
- evidence confidence;
- baseline-classification and early-attempt counts;
- advisory recommendations.

The language is intentionally diagnostic. P28 never says that archived mastery was restored.

## Persistence and authority

P28 adds no database table and no migration.

It derives results from existing persisted evidence:

- study_course_historical_priors;
- study_semesters;
- study_courses;
- study_baseline_diagnostics;
- study_baseline_results;
- study_attempts.

This prevents a second longitudinal truth store from drifting away from the current semester's authoritative evidence.

## Guardrails

P28 must never:

- copy archived mastery into the current course;
- change review_state from a historical-prior result;
- change risk, readiness, weekly allocation, or exam decisions automatically;
- treat guided attempts as transfer evidence;
- validate an early-attempt signal from fewer than four independent attempts;
- infer a durable course trait from prerequisite or related priors;
- infer a durable direct-retake pattern from only one transition;
- treat a contradicted prior as a failure of the current semester;
- claim causal effectiveness from an observational prior match.

## Qualification

P28 is qualified when:

1. positive and negative archived signals can both be confirmed by fresh evidence;
2. reversed current evidence produces a contradicted prior;
3. sparse evidence remains insufficient;
4. insufficient cases do not inflate relation reliability;
5. repeated direct-retake confirmations can form a durable pattern;
6. repeated contradictions become context-sensitive rather than a fixed trait;
7. the Progress UI exposes the result without mutating mastery or scheduling state.
