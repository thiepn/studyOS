### Academic evidence integrity
The old `study_activation_snapshot.majors_with_attempts` field counted course-wide attempts. It could certify a semester even when those attempts were unrelated to Week 1, used hints or were not linked to a verified course source.

The new acceptance read path:
1. Finds the active user's Week-1 teaching week for each configured major.
2. Collects active **verified Week-1 lectures and exercises**; solution-only sources do not substitute for assigned work.
3. Finds source links `study_question_sources` and requires corresponding active questions in that same course.
4. Requires a stored `study_attempts` record on that specific source-linked question with `independence = independent`.
5. Returns the actual counts by course to `/setup`. Only when **every** major has source, question and independent attempt is first-week evidence satisfied.

A hint-assisted attempt or an attempt on an unrelated question does not certify the source-linked requirement. A partial/incorrect independent attempt counts as an **attempt**, not as mastery; the system does not claim to validate the mathematical proof itself. Missing underlying data fails closed. A semester containing zero major courses cannot auto-pass first-week certification.
