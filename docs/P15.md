# P15 — Semester-Level Learning Analytics, Intervention Validation & Structural-Difficulty Detection

## Objective

P14 can detect sustained drift and rebalance a fixed daily study budget. P15 closes the feedback loop:

> Did the correction actually improve later independent performance?

P15 also prevents StudyOS from repeatedly prescribing the same repair when repeated evidence says that repair is not working.

## Intervention identity

A P14 targeted drift-repair session now runs through a dedicated `mode=drift` practice path.

It still uses the existing verified-question/calibration queue and the existing session/attempt infrastructure, but the completed session receives the durable note:

`P14 drift repair intervention`

The session uses the existing `relearning` session type. No new database table or second evidence model is introduced.

Only completed, tagged sessions are treated as P14 interventions by P15.

## Evaluation design

An intervention is evaluated at the teaching-week level.

For an intervention in Week W:

- baseline evidence: independent attempts in W-2 and W-1;
- intervention week: W;
- follow-up evidence: independent attempts in W+1 and W+2;
- attempts from the intervention session itself are excluded from follow-up scoring.

This prevents a successful repair set from being mistaken for durable transfer.

An intervention remains:

- `pending` until at least one later teaching week is complete;
- `insufficient_evidence` unless both baseline and follow-up have at least three independent attempts.

With enough evidence:

- +10 percentage points or more → `effective`;
- -10 percentage points or worse → `regressed`;
- otherwise → `unchanged`.

The thresholds are deliberately conservative. P15 is not allowed to claim success from one good question.

## Course-level effectiveness

For each major course P15 reports:

- completed P14 repairs;
- evaluated repairs;
- effective repairs;
- pending / insufficient-evidence repairs;
- intervention effectiveness rate;
- latest intervention outcome;
- baseline → follow-up accuracy;
- current P14 drift state.

## Difficulty classification

P15 classifies the response pattern, not the student's ability.

### insufficient evidence

There is not enough completed intervention + follow-up evidence.

### transient

A correction worked and the course is back on track. Treat the episode as temporary noise unless it recurs.

### responsive

At least two interventions have enough evidence and at least half improved later independent performance.

### persistent

At least two evaluated corrections have failed to improve performance reliably.

### structural

A course becomes a structural-difficulty signal only when all of the following hold:

- at least three interventions were evaluated;
- no more than one third were effective;
- the course is still drifting or critical;
- the current workload signal is not underinvestment;
- recent accuracy remains weak or unavailable.

This distinction is essential. StudyOS must not call a course structurally difficult merely because too little work was done.

A structural signal means the current **method/course interaction** needs a different strategy, representation, source, or external support. It does not mean the student is incapable of the subject.

## Planner feedback loop

P15 does not increase capacity.

When P14 wants to schedule another targeted repair:

- if P15 has insufficient/transient/responsive evidence, P14 may schedule its normal bounded repair;
- if P15 detects persistent or structural failure, StudyOS stops blindly repeating the same repair;
- the candidate becomes a 15-minute intervention-strategy review linked to the course's evidence on Progress.

Deadlines, retention ceilings, Recovery Mode, and P9 exam strategy remain authoritative.

P16 can build an actual strategy portfolio for these escalated cases.

## UI

### Progress

Progress now contains a semester intervention-validation summary:

- total completed repairs;
- repairs with sufficient follow-up;
- effective repairs;
- pending repairs;
- persistent courses;
- structural-signal courses;
- semester effectiveness rate.

Each major course displays its own intervention history and difficulty signal beside P13 calibration and P14 drift.

### Today

If any course is persistent or structural, Today surfaces the strongest intervention concern and links directly to that course's evidence.

## Architecture

P15 remains derived from existing source-of-truth data:

- `study_sessions`
- `study_attempts`
- `study_week_actions`
- semester/course configuration
- P14's pure drift evaluator

No mastery evidence is created by P15.

## Guardrails

P15 must never:

- count intervention-session performance as proof the intervention worked;
- judge an intervention without enough later independent evidence;
- infer structural difficulty from underinvestment alone;
- add study minutes automatically;
- repeatedly prescribe a failed intervention unchanged;
- override exam strategy or urgent commitments.

## Next phase

**P16 — Strategy Escalation, Intervention Portfolio & Method-Level Experimentation**

P16 should turn persistent/structural signals into controlled alternative interventions: different practice formats, representation changes, source changes, office-hour/tutor escalation, and measured A/B-style method trials without increasing total study capacity.
