# P31 — Core Experience Redesign

## Purpose

P31 makes the P30 academic visual system usable for repeated study: Today → Study → Courses → Progress → More. It does not alter mastery, scheduling ceilings, risk calculations, calendar authority, or semester state.

## The five jobs

### Today — decide and execute

- The bounded daily plan remains the source of truth, with **Now** followed by a short ordered sequence.
- Do not present four decorative KPI cards before the first task.
- Context is a single reading line: capacity mode, reviews due, and weekly commitment.
- The exception list contains decisions that can change today's work: an absent weekly commitment, behind-course allocation, checkpoint, exam, or meaningful intervention.
- If the weekly plan has no detected exceptional signal, do not create a false alarm merely to fill a panel.
- Calendar/deadline and capacity controls remain secondary drawers.

### Study — solve without interface noise

- Active sessions may enter focus mode with the ordinary page navigation concealed. A visible **Show navigation** control can restore it.
- The question has explicit progress/time context, with no study-mode analytics nearby.
- Students can choose **Type here** or **Work on paper**. Paper mode skips the large text field but retains the same answer-lock and rubric workflow.
- Ctrl/Cmd+Enter locks a typed answer; hints and giving up continue to alter evidence strength exactly as before.
- Grading, confidence, and error choice controls expose their selection state with `aria-pressed`.
- All session event recording, fallback sync, and mastery eligibility remain unchanged.

### Courses — do the next academic action

- Course names open their own binders directly.
- Course detail opens with the weekly working loop, not configuration or exam analytics.
- The earliest unfinished actionable teaching week receives the initial expanded position.
- Retained and unreleased weeks cannot displace executable work.
- Other weeks remain accessible as expandable rows with all original resources, milestones, and findings.
- Master map, exam intelligence, and course settings remain accessible after the weekly work.

### Progress — explain rather than overwhelm

- Start with a short semester overview and course-level condition.
- Per course, first show only tested skills, due/at-risk skills, exam-readiness evidence, and one evidence-based next recommendation.
- Overdue detail, calibration, drift, interventions, historical transfer, risk drivers, and operating mix remain accessible in a course disclosure.
- Cross-course intervention and historical metrics move to a deeper separate disclosure after the course ledger.
- There is no new readiness score or prediction model.

### More — real destination, not a menu overlay

- `/more` is a complete page with direct, grouped links to Plan, Materials, Exams, Semester, and System.
- It replaces the previous floating dropdown navigation.
- All prior routes remain directly addressable and functional; their active navigation category is More.
- `/outlook` and `/quality` remain contextual Progress views, and course diagnostics remain in the Courses family.

## Behavior and compatibility requirements

- No study logic or database migration in P31.
- No new AI features, no gamification, no dashboard-style hero.
- Course visual identity from P30 is unchanged.
- The P29 navigation taxonomy remains in force: Today, Study, Courses, Progress, More.
- Mobile remains touch-first with the real More page as the fifth bottom navigation destination.
- Existing URLs, saved attempts, timestamps, and academic evidence are not reinterpreted.

## Verification

- Tests cover the deterministic featured-week selection and More route taxonomy.
- Run behavior tests, TypeScript, and a production build.
- A production visual/device QA pass still belongs to P34/P39 and requires a live, instrumented deployment.
- P31 is about interaction architecture; no claim of final device-verified polish is made.
