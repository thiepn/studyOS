# P16 — Strategy Escalation, Intervention Portfolio & Method-Level Experimentation

## Objective

P15 can tell when repeated P14 repairs are not transferring. P16 changes the question from:

> “Should this course get another repair?”

to:

> “Which different learning mechanism should we test next, and did that method transfer?”

P16 never increases the total daily study budget.

## Method portfolio

P16 has seven in-app method families:

1. **Concept reconstruction** — rebuild definitions, theorem assumptions, and relationships from memory.
2. **Method discrimination** — choose and justify the method before calculation/proof execution.
3. **Worked-example fading** — begin independently, then use minimal hints rather than full solutions.
4. **Timed execution** — improve fluency and clean execution under a short time constraint.
5. **Interleaved transfer** — mix different skills so method recognition and transfer are required.
6. **Prerequisite repair** — prioritize high-leverage foundational skills.
7. **Oral explanation** — explain the concept/method aloud before writing the solution.

These are not seven difficulty settings. They target different failure mechanisms.

## Strategy selection

The pure strategy selector uses:

- P15 difficulty state;
- P13 dominant recurring error;
- P13 weakest evidence dimension;
- P13 pace ratio;
- recent P14 accuracy;
- prior P16 experiment outcomes.

A strategy gets extra preference when its target errors/dimensions match the current signal.

A previously effective method may be reused. A method with two evaluated experiments and zero successful transfer outcomes is **retired**.

## Controlled experimentation

A P16 experiment is a normal StudyOS `relearning` session with a durable note:

`P16 strategy experiment:<strategy_key>`

The session uses only verified existing questions. Its own attempts are excluded from transfer validation.

P16 reuses P15's later-transfer evaluator:

- baseline: normal independent evidence before the experiment;
- intervention: the tagged P16 session;
- follow-up: later normal independent evidence;
- all P14/P16 intervention-session attempts are excluded from baseline/follow-up.

This keeps the outcome about transfer rather than practice-set success.

## One unresolved experiment at a time

If any method experiment is still pending or lacks enough follow-up evidence, StudyOS blocks another P16 experiment.

Normal coursework and review continue.

This prevents method A and method B from overlapping so heavily that later improvement cannot be attributed to either method.

## Strategy status

Each method is classified:

- **untested** — never run;
- **testing** — not enough reliable evidence yet;
- **promising** — at least one effective transfer outcome;
- **proven** — at least two evaluated runs with a strong effectiveness rate;
- **retired** — at least two evaluated runs with zero effective transfer outcomes.

Retired methods are removed from automatic recommendation.

## Escalation beyond in-app practice

P16 has two higher escalation states.

### Change source

When a structural course has already retired several distinct methods, the next experiment should use a different explanation source: another textbook, lecturer explanation, tutorial sheet, reference, or representation.

### External support

If every in-app method family has been retired, StudyOS stops cycling self-practice and recommends taking the precise failed skills/errors to a tutorial, office hour, lecturer, tutor, or knowledgeable peer.

This is deliberately preferable to automatically adding hours.

## Planner integration

When P15 marks a course persistent or structural:

- the P14 “repeat another repair” path is replaced;
- Today gets a bounded **strategy escalation** candidate;
- the candidate opens the P16 Strategy Lab;
- the lab recommends a 15–20 minute controlled method experiment;
- that experiment remains inside the existing P10 daily capacity.

Deadlines, review ceilings, Recovery Mode, and P9 exam strategy remain authoritative.

## UI

### Strategy Lab

For each major course:

- current P15 difficulty signal;
- recommended method;
- reason;
- method portfolio/status;
- run count;
- evaluated count;
- transfer effectiveness;
- retired/proven state;
- experiment history;
- baseline → follow-up accuracy;
- source/support escalation when needed.

### Practice

A method experiment has an explicit strategy-specific instruction and is tagged for later validation.

### Today / Progress

Persistent/structural cases link directly into P16 instead of telling the user merely to inspect the failed P15 evidence.

## Guardrails

P16 must never:

- run two unresolved method experiments in the same course;
- use experiment-session performance as proof of transfer;
- keep recommending a method after repeated failed transfer;
- infer external-support need from one failed session;
- increase total study capacity automatically;
- override deadlines or exam strategy;
- generate fake practice when no verified questions exist.
