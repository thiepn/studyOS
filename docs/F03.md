# F03 — Account Experience & Recovery

## Boundaries
F03 stacks on qualified F02. It does not use or transfer the ChatGPT connector's OAuth tokens, recreate THIEPN Account identity, write Google events, provision a second Drive, or change academic records.

## Implemented code
- A pre-semester, authenticated `/account` control center reads owner-scoped StudyOS connection metadata, separating THIEPN Account, Google Drive, Google Calendar.
- Error, missing, disconnected, and connected states have safe recovery text. Raw provider exceptions and tokens are not shown.
- Reconnect/switch actions are explicit. Account changes require a same-origin POST intent, bound to the signed-in THIEPN owner, before the Google OAuth callback may replace the account. Same-account re-consent works without a switch intent.
- Calendar switch/disconnect is blocked when locally committed study blocks still need reconciliation; provider events are never silently deleted or orphaned.
- The Calendar primary/source selection is preserved for same-account reauthorization; a real account change starts with the new account's primary source.
- Existing Drive IDs and study materials are not deleted. A Drive switch resets *database mapping* only after explicit approval, and the app's normal provisioning can adopt preexisting matching folder names.
- Provider token revocation is exposed as a reconnect state. Failures do not equate to lost academic data.

## Certification boundaries
Automated source, test, typecheck, production build and unauthenticated server-smoke gates are mandatory. No claim of a completed Google login/consent or two-account privacy/browser/device certification absent corresponding direct human evidence.
