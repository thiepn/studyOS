# P20 — Weekly Execution Quality, Plan Adherence & Allocation Calibration

## Objective

P18 decides how a fixed weekly envelope should be distributed. P19 makes that envelope operational. P20 asks whether the planning heuristics themselves match real semester behavior.

P20 answers four separate questions:

1. **Whole-week realism** — are committed course budgets repeatedly too large or too small?
2. **Course allocation quality** — is a specific course repeatedly receiving more or less time than execution supports?
3. **Protection fairness** — is the same course repeatedly sacrificed when capacity is tight?
4. **Estimate quality** — when StudyOS has paired planned/actual session minutes, are estimates systematically biased?

These questions are intentionally kept separate. Missing one course target is not automatically treated as evidence that the course should receive fewer minutes.

## Evidence source

P20 introduces no new persistence.

It derives calibration from:

- completed, non-cancelled P19 weekly plans;
- original and current P19 course envelopes;
- stored P19 protection floors;
- credited course work from existing `study_sessions` and workflow milestones;
- P19 rebalance revisions;
- paired `planned_minutes` / `actual_minutes` study-session evidence.

Current partial weeks are excluded from calibration.

A past plan whose end date has passed is treated as completed evidence even if its stored status still says `active`. This avoids partial-current-week contamination without requiring analytics reads to mutate plan rows.

## Credited execution

P20 reuses P19's conservative execution model:

> course credited minutes = max(recorded session minutes, workflow milestone credit)

Session and workflow minutes are not added together.

For whole-week adherence, course credited minutes are allowed to exceed the target. That is necessary to detect repeated under-allocation.

## Calibration evidence gate

Per-course floor calibration is gated by completed-week count:

- 0–1 weeks: **insufficient**
- 2 weeks: **emerging**
- 3–4 weeks: **usable**
- 5+ weeks: **established**

No P18 floor changes are allowed before three completed observations for that course.

## Course allocation signals

### Underallocated

A week is an under-allocation signal when credited course work exceeds the target by at least:

- 30 minutes, or
- 20% of the target,

whichever is larger.

A course becomes systematically underallocated after at least two such weeks within a 3+ week evidence set.

### Overallocated

A course miss is only considered course-specific overallocation when:

- target is at least 45 minutes;
- credited work is short by at least 30 minutes or 25% of target; and
- the week as a whole reached at least 85% of its course budget.

This prevents a globally failed week from being misread as evidence against one course.

A course becomes systematically overallocated after at least two qualifying weeks.

### Volatile

If a course has repeated over- and under-allocation signals, P20 labels it volatile and does not auto-adjust the floor.

## Repeated sacrifice

A course counts as sacrificed if either:

- its original P19 allocation was below its stored protection floor, or
- its final rebalanced target remained below that floor.

This means a later rebalance cannot erase evidence that the course was initially starved.

Two or more sacrifice weeks within a usable evidence set add protection rather than normalizing the starvation pattern.

## Session estimate calibration

Only real pairs where both `planned_minutes` and `actual_minutes` exist are used.

At least four paired sessions are required.

Median actual/planned ratio:

- < 0.80 → **overestimated**
- 0.80–1.19 → **accurate**
- >= 1.20 → **underestimated**

Estimate bias is diagnostic. It strengthens an existing course-floor correction but does not directly rewrite P10 task durations in P20.

## Automatic floor correction

P20 changes only the P18/P19 **course protection floor**.

Adjustments occur in 15-minute steps and are capped to **−30 / +30 minutes**.

Possible positive evidence:

- repeated under-allocation: +15;
- repeated sacrifice: +15;
- under-allocation plus underestimated session durations: another +15, subject to cap.

Possible negative evidence:

- repeated course-specific overallocation: −15;
- if the course is also strongly ready and session durations are overestimated: another −15.

Guardrails:

- fewer than three completed course-weeks → 0;
- a weak-readiness course is never reduced merely because its target was missed;
- repeated sacrifice can never produce a negative correction;
- volatile patterns produce no correction;
- final adjustment is capped to ±30;
- calibrated protection floor itself remains bounded to 0–240 minutes.

## Whole-week capacity signal

P20 separately evaluates final course-budget adherence across completed weeks.

After at least three completed weeks:

- repeated <75% adherence with a low median → **commitment too high**;
- repeated >115% adherence with a high median → **commitment too low**;
- both high and low weeks → **volatile**;
- otherwise → **aligned**.

The resulting ±10% capacity suggestion is **advisory only**.

P20 does not automatically alter:

- P10 Normal/Light/Recovery/Intensive daily settings;
- P11 Calendar availability;
- mandatory commitments;
- retention reserve.

This is necessary because course-envelope adherence does not measure every minute spent on deadlines and retention.

## P18 integration

`ScenarioInput` now accepts a per-course `floorAdjustments` map.

P20 adjustments are:

- rounded to 15-minute blocks;
- capped at ±30 minutes;
- added to the existing P18 floor;
- visible in P18 reason text and course metrics.

All P18 objectives and capacity stress tests use the same calibrated floors.

The browser-side P18 slider receives the same adjustment map, preventing calibration from disappearing when a scenario is recomputed client-side.

## P19 integration

New weekly commitments use calibrated P18 floors.

Rolling P19 reallocation also receives the current calibration map, so the active weekly control loop does not silently fall back to uncalibrated floors.

P19's original allocation remains preserved for audit.

## UI

New route:

`/quality`

It shows:

- completed-week evidence count;
- evidence maturity;
- median course-budget adherence;
- capacity realism signal;
- rebalance frequency;
- total sacrifice events;
- per-course allocation signal;
- original/final target adherence;
- sacrifice count;
- estimate sample count;
- median actual/planned ratio;
- estimate signal;
- active floor correction;
- completed-week history.

The `/week` view includes a compact P20 calibration summary.

The P18 scenario view explicitly marks active P20 floor corrections.

## Architecture

P20 is derived and stateless.

No new Supabase table, view, RPC, or RLS policy is introduced.

The existing P19 persistence is sufficient because it already preserves:

- weekly boundaries;
- original targets;
- final targets;
- protection floors;
- revision history indicator;
- course readiness snapshot.

## Authority boundaries

P20 may calibrate P18 protection floors.

P20 must not:

- change P17 readiness;
- change P9 exam strategy;
- change P10 daily capacity automatically;
- change P11 calendar free time;
- expand a P19 weekly commitment;
- reinterpret whole-week failure as course-specific overallocation;
- reduce weak-course protection solely because execution missed the target;
- use current partial-week data for calibration;
- infer causal learning benefit from adherence alone.

## Current-semester cold start

Immediately after P20 ships, there may be zero completed P19 weeks.

That is expected.

The UI must say that calibration evidence is insufficient and leave all floor adjustments at zero until real completed weeks accumulate.

## Next phase

**P21 — Closed-Loop Weekly Review, Next-Week Handoff & Semester Adaptation**

P21 should turn the completed P19/P20 week into an explicit review-and-handoff flow: summarize what happened, explain calibration changes, carry unresolved real commitments without manufacturing debt, generate the next P18 scenario from fresh P17/P20 evidence, and require explicit commitment of the new week.
